Coding features, reviewing code, pushing to prod… it’s useful, it brings value to users and it guarantees quality. But… where’s the fun in all that?

Do you also come across super kawaii libs that make you want to play with them, but never find the time to try them out? Then for 15 minutes, let’s treat ourselves: take some time just to have fun.

On the agenda: confetti, sound, crazy effects… in short, everything we can squeeze into a web app to give our users a guaranteed ✨ amazing ✨ experience!
